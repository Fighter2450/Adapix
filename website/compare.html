An honest comparison

How Adapix compares to the tools you’ve heard of

The other tools are good at what they do — that’s the honest truth. They’re just built for a different job. Here’s what each one is actually for, so you can pick right (even if that isn’t us).

vs. review & messaging platforms

The tools known for review requests and a shared inbox.

What they’re built for Collecting reviews and centralizing inbound messages. If your #1 problem is your Google rating, they’re a solid pick — that’s their home turf.
What Adapix is built for Going after the money you already quoted. Adapix watches for quotes that went quiet and missed calls, writes the follow-up itself — text, email, or an actual phone call — and sends it after your one-tap approval. Review requests are included, but recovery is the job.

Honest take: if you mainly want reviews and a unified inbox, they’re great. If you want the follow-up done for you, that’s us.

vs. all-in-one marketing suites

The build-anything platforms with funnels, pipelines, and automations.

What they’re built for Agencies and marketing-savvy operators who want to build campaigns, funnels, and workflows. Enormous power — if you have the hours (or hire someone) to set it up and run it.
What Adapix is built for Owners who don’t want to build anything. There’s no funnel editor and no workflow canvas. You import your customers, and drafts start appearing in your inbox for approval. Setup is minutes, not weekends.

Honest take: if you enjoy marketing tools or have an agency running one for you, a suite gives you more knobs. If the knobs are why nothing ever gets sent, you want fewer knobs.

vs. your field-service software’s add-ons

The scheduling/invoicing platform you may already use, with its built-in reminders.

What they’re built for Running the job: scheduling, dispatch, invoices. Their messaging is mostly fixed templates — appointment reminders, invoice nudges — around work that’s already booked.
What Adapix is built for The customers who haven’t booked yet. Adapix writes a personal message that names the actual job and price (“still want that water heater in before winter?”), handles the reply, and remembers the details for next time. It works alongside your field-service software, not instead of it.

Honest take: keep your field-service software — it’s good at running jobs. Adapix hunts the jobs that went quiet before they ever hit your schedule.

vs. doing it yourself

Free, in theory You know your customers best, and a personal text from you converts brilliantly. The problem was never quality — it’s that follow-up is the first thing that falls off when you’re busy, and busy is exactly when the most quotes are out.
You, with a memory that never slips Adapix writes it like you — your voice, the real job, the real price — and you still read and approve every word. You stay the sender; you just stop being the bottleneck.

Honest take: if you reliably follow up with every quote within a day, you don’t need us. Almost nobody does.
