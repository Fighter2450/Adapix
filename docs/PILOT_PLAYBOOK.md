# Pilot playbook — 5 real customers, 5 real testimonials

_The single highest-leverage thing for Adapix right now. Nobody buys AI that texts their
customers from a company with zero users on record. Five named pilots with real recovered-dollar
stories flips every "no" on the landing page. This is the founder's job — nobody can outsource
the first five._

## The offer (simple, no haggling)
> "I'll run Adapix for your business **free for 60 days**, set it up for you personally,
> and be your support line directly. In exchange: if it wins you back real jobs, you let me
> use your first name, business type, and the real numbers in a testimonial — and take one
> 15-minute feedback call."

Rules:
- **Free, not discounted.** A discount invites negotiation; free invites a yes.
- **You do the setup.** Import their customer file yourself on a screen-share. Their effort ≈ 20 minutes.
- **Cap it at 5.** Scarcity is honest here — you genuinely can't hand-hold more.
- Payment isn't the goal; the *story* is. (Also solves the Stripe-test-mode gap: pilots don't pay.)

## Who to target (in order of likelihood to say yes)
1. **People you already know** — family, friends-of-family, your barber, anyone who runs or
   works at a service business. Warm intro beats everything.
2. **Local Pittsburgh trades** — plumbers, HVAC, electricians, landscapers, detailers, cleaners.
   Small enough that the owner answers their own phone.
3. **Businesses that just quoted YOU (or your family) and never followed up** — the perfect
   opener: "you quoted us three weeks ago and never followed up — that's literally the problem
   my software fixes."
- Skip: franchises (corporate rules), medical (compliance overhead for a pilot), anyone with a
  full-time office manager who already does follow-up well.

## Outreach templates

**Text / DM (warm-ish contact):**
> Hey [name] — Rocco here. I built a piece of software that chases the quotes that go quiet
> (the "let me think about it" people) and I need 5 real businesses to prove it on. Free for
> 60 days, I set everything up myself. If it wins you back jobs, you let me quote the numbers.
> Worst case you got free follow-up for 2 months. Interested?

**Cold email (local trade):**
> Subject: 5 dead quotes
>
> Hi [name] — I'm Rocco, I build software here in Pittsburgh. Quick question: how many quotes
> did you send last month that just… went quiet?
>
> My product (Adapix) writes the follow-up for each one — text, email, or a call, in your
> voice — and nothing sends until you tap approve. I'm taking 5 local businesses free for
> 60 days; I do all the setup. All I ask is a testimonial if it works.
>
> The fastest way to see it: send me your five deadest quotes and I'll show you the five
> follow-ups it writes. 15 minutes, worst case you keep the messages.
>
> — Rocco · adapixai.com

**The counter-objection you'll hear:** "I don't want AI texting my customers."
> "It can't. Every message waits in an inbox until you read it and tap approve — I'll show
> you on the first one. You stay the sender; it just does the remembering."

## Running each pilot (60 days)
- **Day 0:** 30-min screen-share. Import their customer file, fill Business Knowledge
  (services, prices, hours), send the first 5 follow-ups WITH them watching. They must feel
  the approve-tap themselves.
- **Week 1:** check in every other day. Fix anything janky same-day — pilot feedback is a
  bug list from people who owe you honesty.
- **Weekly:** send them their own numbers (sent / replies / jobs won back). Owners forget
  wins; the log becomes the testimonial.
- **Day 45:** the ask, verbatim: "Can I quote that? First name, business type, and the number."
- **Day 60:** offer founding pricing to convert ($49/mo — matches the founding-deal note in
  MARKETING.md) or part as friends. Either way you keep the story.

## What a testimonial must contain (or it's wallpaper)
1. Real first name + trade + town ("Dave, plumber, Pittsburgh") — with written permission.
2. A number ("won back $3,400 in the first month" / "4 dead quotes replied").
3. The fear it beat ("I thought AI would embarrass me — then I saw every message waits for my OK").

Never invent, round up, or "improve" one. One real modest number beats five glossy fakes —
and fake ones violate MARKETING.md and can kill the company's trust permanently.

## Where the proof goes (once real)
- Landing page: 3 quote cards under "See it in action" + numbers in the hero.
- The comparison page's closing section.
- Social: each testimonial becomes a demonstration post (screenshot the real reply thread
  with the customer's name blurred, with the pilot's permission).
- The demo video's end card: "[Dave], plumber — $X won back."

## Weekly cadence until 5 are signed
- 10 outreach touches/week minimum (5 warm, 5 cold). Track in a simple sheet:
  name / trade / channel / date / status / next touch.
- Follow up on every non-reply after 3 days — you of all people can't let outreach go quiet.
